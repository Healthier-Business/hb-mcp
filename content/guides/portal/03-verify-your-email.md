# Verify your email

Right after sign-up (and every time you try to sign in before confirming) you see the **Check your inbox** page. It exists to prove that the address belongs to you. Nothing in the console works until you have used the link.

## Confirm the address

1. **Open the email**

   Look for a message from the portal's sender address with a button that confirms your address. It usually arrives within a minute.

2. **Choose the confirmation link**

   The link is personal and signed. It stays valid for **3 days**. Opening it confirms the address and takes you to the sign-in page with the notice *Your email is confirmed. You can sign in.*

3. **Sign in**

   Sign in with your password. The console home page greets you and shows the [getting started checklist](hb://guides/portal-the-console-tour), where *Confirm your email* is now ticked.

## If the email does not arrive

1. Look in the spam or junk folder, and in a quarantine area of your company mail system. Messages from a new sender are often held there.
2. Check the address on the page. If you made a typo, create the account again with the correct address; the first one can be left alone.
3. Choose **Send the email again**. A fresh link is sent.
4. Ask your mail administrator to allow the sender, then try again.

> **Warning: You can ask for the email only three times in ten minutes.**
>
> After three requests the page answers *You asked for several emails already. Wait a few minutes, then try again.* The older links keep working until they expire, so look for all of them before asking again.

## Good to know

* **Sign-in before confirmation** shows this page instead of the console. No second email is sent by signing in.
* **The page remembers the address for your browser session only.** If you close the browser and come back, sign in again and you are returned here.
* **A link that expired** (after 3 days) takes you to the sign-in page with *That confirmation link has expired. Sign in to get a new one.* Sign in; you see the Check your inbox page again and can ask for a new email.
* **An approval portal** still needs approval after confirmation: the notice then reads *Your email is confirmed. The team that runs this portal will review your account.*
